Overview

What We Offer

Custom drapery is one of the most transformative elements in any interior. Floor-to-ceiling panels hung correctly can make a room feel taller, more refined, and more complete than almost any other single design decision. Done wrong, they make a space feel cheap regardless of everything else in the room.

At Navigate, we design, fabricate, and install custom drapery panels in a curated selection of designer fabrics — with architectural hardware specified to complement your interior's finish palette. Every detail, from rod placement to hem break to pleat style, is considered and executed with precision.

Common Questions

Everything You Need
to Know

Should drapery hang from ceiling to floor?

For maximum elegance in a luxury home, yes — floor-to-ceiling panels hung as close to the ceiling as possible create the most architecturally refined look. We mount hardware at the correct height for your specific ceiling and window to achieve this effect.

What is ripple fold drapery?

Ripple fold is a modern pleat style where panels hang in consistent, evenly spaced waves across a track system. It is the most popular choice for contemporary and coastal modern interiors — clean, minimal, and beautifully architectural.

Can drapery be combined with other window treatments?

Absolutely. Layering drapery with roller shades or Roman shades beneath is one of the most effective design approaches — the shades provide light control and privacy while the drapery adds softness, warmth, and decorative finish.
