Overview

What We Offer

South Florida's outdoor living spaces are as important as any room inside the home — and without proper shading, they are unusable for a significant portion of the year. Motorized outdoor shades transform your patio, lanai, or pergola into a genuinely comfortable, year-round living space.

At Navigate, we specify and install motorized exterior shading systems engineered specifically for Florida's climate — built to withstand UV intensity, salt air, humidity, and the wind loads that coastal locations demand. They protect your outdoor furniture, reduce interior solar gain, and dramatically extend the usability of your outdoor spaces.

Why Navigate

The Details That
Make the Difference

Wind-Rated Systems

Our outdoor shade systems are specified with appropriate wind ratings for coastal Florida locations — including systems with automatic wind sensors that retract shades when wind speeds exceed safe operating thresholds.

UV & Heat Reduction

Exterior shading is significantly more effective at reducing solar heat gain than interior treatments — blocking the sun before it enters the glass rather than after. Our outdoor systems reduce patio temperatures by up to 20°F.

Motorized Operation

All outdoor shade systems we install are motorized — manual operation of large exterior screens is impractical. We integrate with your existing smart-home system or provide standalone remote and app control.

Salt Air & Weather Resistant

Every component — fabric, hardware, motor, and mounting — is specified for coastal environments. Salt air corrosion and UV degradation destroy inferior outdoor products within seasons. Ours are built to last.

View-Through & Privacy Screens

Choose from view-through solar fabrics that preserve your outdoor sightlines while blocking sun and insects, or solid privacy screens for complete enclosure. Multiple opacity levels available.

Patio, Lanai & Pergola Systems

We install side screens, drop shades, pergola cover systems, and full outdoor room enclosures — custom engineered for your specific outdoor space dimensions and architecture.

Common Questions

Everything You Need
to Know

Are outdoor shades hurricane rated?

The outdoor shading systems we install are designed for everyday weather and elevated wind conditions, but are not rated as hurricane protection products. We always recommend retracting outdoor shades before tropical storm or hurricane conditions.

What is the best fabric for outdoor shades in Florida?

We specify PVC-coated polyester fabrics with UV inhibitors and salt-air resistant coatings for all outdoor applications. These fabrics maintain color stability, resist mold and mildew, and withstand Florida's climate year after year.

Can outdoor shades reduce mosquitoes and insects?

Screen fabrics with very fine mesh openness factors (1% or less) provide meaningful insect reduction while maintaining airflow and views. We can specify screens specifically for this purpose.
