Common Questions

Everything You Need
to Know

How long does motorized shade installation take?

Most whole-home motorized shade installations are completed in one day. Larger projects may require two days. We coordinate everything in advance so your home is minimally disrupted.

What happens if the power goes out?

All motorized shades we install include a manual override option. Battery backup motors are also available for critical applications.

Can motorized shades be added to an existing smart-home system?

Yes — we specialize in integrating motorized shades into existing Lutron, Control4, Crestron, and other smart-home platforms. We work directly with your AV integrator when needed.

What is the difference between Lutron and Somfy?

Both are premium motorization systems. Lutron is the preferred choice for deep smart-home integration with its own lighting ecosystem. Somfy is excellent for standalone motorization and wider product compatibility. We will recommend the right system for your specific home and goals.
