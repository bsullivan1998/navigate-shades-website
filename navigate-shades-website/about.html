Who We Are

Not a Franchise.
A Concierge.

Navigate Window & Shade Co. was founded on a single conviction: the homes of South Florida deserve a window treatment experience as refined as the architecture they're built into.

We are not a franchise. We are not a big-box alternative. We are a boutique design studio — personally curating every product specification, personally consulting every client, and personally ensuring every installation exceeds expectation.

Our process begins where most companies stop — with a deep understanding of your home's architecture, your lifestyle requirements, and your design vision. Only then do we begin specifying product.

The result is a shading system that doesn't just look beautiful on installation day. It functions beautifully for the life of your home.
