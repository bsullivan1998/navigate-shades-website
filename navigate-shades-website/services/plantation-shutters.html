Plantation Shutters

Overview

What We Offer

Plantation shutters are the quintessential coastal window treatment — and for good reason. They manage Florida's intense sunlight beautifully, provide genuine privacy control, withstand humidity and salt air, and add architectural permanence to a home's interior that no other treatment can replicate.

At Navigate, we specify and install custom plantation shutters in both genuine hardwood and premium composite materials — fabricated to your exact window dimensions and finished in the color that integrates seamlessly with your interior architecture.

The Details That

Hardwood & Composite Options

We offer genuine hardwood shutters for interior spaces and premium composite shutters for high-humidity areas — bathrooms, kitchens, and any space with direct moisture exposure. Both options are fabricated to exact specifications.

Custom Louvre Sizing

Louvre size affects both the look and the light control of your shutters. We offer 2.5", 3.5", and 4.5" louvre options and help you select the right size for your window proportions and design intent.

Full Frame or L-Frame

We install shutters with full frames or L-frames depending on your window type and mounting preference — ensuring a precise, built-in appearance that looks like it belongs architecturally.

Coastal Durability

For South Florida's coastal environment, we specify shutters with moisture-resistant finishes and salt-air-resistant hardware — materials that maintain their beauty and function in conditions that degrade inferior products.

Specialty Shapes

Arched windows, angled tops, and specialty shapes are no problem. We fabricate custom shutter panels for any window geometry your home presents.

Precise Installation

Shutter installation requires the same precision as fine cabinetry — level frames, consistent gap reveals, smooth operation. Our installation team treats every shutter project with this standard.

Common Questions

Everything You Need
to Know

Are plantation shutters appropriate for Florida's humidity?

With the right material selection, absolutely. We specify moisture-resistant composite shutters for bathrooms and other humid spaces, and genuine hardwood with sealed finishes for interior living areas. Both are formulated to withstand Florida's climate.

How do plantation shutters affect energy efficiency?

Plantation shutters provide a genuine insulating layer at the window — particularly when closed, they create a dead air space that reduces heat transfer. They also allow precise control of solar gain through louvre adjustment.

Can plantation shutters be installed on sliding glass doors?

Yes. Bypass shutter systems are designed specifically for sliding door openings. Bifold shutter systems are also available and work beautifully on wide door openings.
