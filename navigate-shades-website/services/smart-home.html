Common Questions

Everything You Need
to Know

What is the difference between Lutron RadioRA3 and Caseta?

Caseta is Lutron's entry-level smart home system — excellent for smaller applications and budget-conscious projects. RadioRA3 is Lutron's mid-range professional system with larger device capacity, more sophisticated programming, and stronger professional support. Homeworks QSX is Lutron's flagship system for the most demanding luxury residential projects.

Can smart shading be added to an existing home?

Yes. Most of our smart shading installations are retrofits into existing homes. Lutron's radio frequency systems require no new wiring, making integration into existing construction straightforward.

Can I control my shades with voice commands?

Yes. Lutron, Somfy, and most Control4/Crestron systems integrate with Amazon Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit — allowing full voice control of your shades alongside your other smart home devices.
