Roman Shades

Overview

What We Offer

Roman shades bring warmth and dimension to a window that hard treatments and roller shades simply cannot replicate. The soft horizontal folds create a sculptural quality that elevates any room — from coastal casual to formal living spaces.

At Navigate, every Roman shade is custom fabricated in our curated fabric collection, with precise attention to fold depth, fabric weight, and lining selection. The result is a treatment that looks as considered as the room it finishes.

The Details That
Make the Difference

Custom Fabricated

Every Roman shade is made to your exact window measurements with consistent fold depth and precise hem alignment — no two are identical because no two windows are.

Fabric & Lining Selection

We guide you through fabric selection for weight, texture, pattern scale, and light filtration. Lining options include unlined, privacy, blackout, and thermal for energy efficiency.

Flat, Relaxed & Hobbled Styles

Choose from flat Roman shades for a clean architectural look, relaxed Romans for a casual coastal feel, or hobbled Romans for a more traditional layered profile.

Motorization Available

Roman shades can be motorized for hands-free operation — particularly valuable for high windows, large formats, or whole-home automation integration.

Coastal Fabric Curation

Our fabric selection is curated with South Florida interiors in mind — linens, performance fabrics, and textures that work with coastal light and humidity without fading or warping.

Expert Installation

Properly installed Romans hang perfectly flat, fold evenly, and operate smoothly. Our installation team takes the same precision approach to soft treatments as to hard ones.

Common Questions

Everything You Need
to Know

Are Roman shades appropriate for humid coastal environments?

Yes, with the right fabric selection. We specifically recommend performance fabrics and treated linens for coastal homes — materials that resist moisture, salt air, and UV degradation.

Can Roman shades be used in kitchens and bathrooms?

Yes. We specify moisture-resistant fabrics for high-humidity applications. Faux linen and performance weaves work beautifully in kitchens and master baths.

How are Roman shades measured and hung?

We measure each window individually. Roman shades can be inside mounted for a clean architectural look or outside mounted to maximize light blockage and create the illusion of larger windows.
