The Residential Experience

Your Home,
Perfected.

Navigate Window & Shade Co. is South Florida's fully mobile luxury window treatment consultancy. We don't have a showroom — because your home is our showroom. Every consultation, every sample presentation, every design proposal happens in your space, with your light, against your specific architecture.

We built the business this way intentionally. Seeing a fabric swatch under fluorescent showroom lighting tells you almost nothing about how it will behave in your coastal home at 4pm on a sunny afternoon. We believe the only place to design a window treatment system is in the home it will live in.

The result is a shading system that functions beautifully, integrates seamlessly with your smart-home, and looks like it was always meant to be there — because it was designed there.

Who We Serve

Built for South Florida's
Finest Homes

◈

Waterfront Estates

Intracoastal and oceanfront homes present unique challenges — intense direct sun, salt air, privacy from water, and large glass expanses. We specialize in exactly this environment.

◈

Coastal Modern Homes

Contemporary and coastal modern architecture demands window treatments that are architecturally considered — clean lines, minimal hardware, and precise proportions that complement the design.

◈

New Construction

We partner with luxury custom home builders throughout the Treasure Coast and Palm Beach County as a preferred trade partner — specifying and installing complete window treatment packages for new builds.

◈

Luxury Condominiums

High-rise and low-rise luxury condominiums require treatments that manage intense sun, respect HOA guidelines, and work within the architectural constraints of the building. We handle all of this.

◈

Smart-Home Residences

For homes with Lutron, Control4, Crestron, or other automation platforms, we integrate motorized shading seamlessly — adding shade scenes, schedules, and automations that complete the smart-home experience.

◈

Interior Designer Projects

We work alongside interior designers as a fully mobile trade partner — visiting client homes on your behalf, presenting samples in the actual space, and delivering proposals that reflect your design intent and your client's architecture.
