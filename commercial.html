Commercial Space

Navigate Window & Shade Co. brings the same level of design intent, product quality, and installation precision to commercial projects that we apply to South Florida's finest residences.

We work with property managers, commercial developers, hospitality operators, medical and professional offices, and luxury retail environments — specifying and installing window treatment systems that enhance the space, improve energy efficiency, and create a refined environment for clients and employees alike.
